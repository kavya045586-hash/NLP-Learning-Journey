

## 🌐 What is NLP?
- NLP enables machines to understand human language (text or speech).
- Examples:
  - Google search: "Weather in Delhi"
  - Alexa: "Play Arijit Singh songs"

---

## 💡 Real-World Applications
- Chatbots & Virtual Assistants (ChatGPT, Alexa, Siri, Google Assistant)
- Speech recognition
- Text understanding
- Response generation

---

## 🛠️ Approaches of NLP
### 1. Rule-Based Approach (Old School)
- Relies on predefined grammar/rules.
- **Limitations:** Not scalable, fails with ambiguity or new patterns.

### 2. Statistical (Machine Learning) Approach
- Treats language as data → algorithms learn patterns.
- **Models:** Naive Bayes, Logistic Regression, SVM.
- **Feature Extraction:** Bag of Words, TF-IDF.
- **Use Cases:** Sentiment analysis, spam detection, text classification.
- **Limitations:** Loses context (sarcasm, word sequence).

### 3. Deep Learning-Based Approach (Modern NLP)
- Neural networks capture meaning, context, and word order.
- **Architectures:** RNN, LSTM, GRU, Transformers (BERT, GPT, T5).
- **Feature Representation:** Word embeddings (Word2Vec, GloVe) or token IDs with embedding layers.
